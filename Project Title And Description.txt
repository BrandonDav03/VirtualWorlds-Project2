SIC Ears!

This world, entitled SIC Ears, is a late beginner/early intermediate ear training tool for musicians. SIC stands for Scales, Intervals, Chords; those are the three skills being trained. The player spawns in a sort of hub area, surrounded by three different hallways, one for each skill, each of which connects to a room with two pianos in it. The pianos are labeled "Practice" and "Play." The practice piano has a button for each type of scale, interval, or chord that the simulation covers. For example, the practice chords piano has a button for Major 7, Dominant 7, Minor 7, Half-Diminished 7, and Diminished 7 chords. When pressed, the piano will simply play the sound corresponding to the button. The play piano has all of the same buttons as the practice piano, but in addition it has a button to start the game. When pressed, it will play a random one of the corresponding sounds, and the player then has 10 seconds to click the correct button.
